VECT and TACT show that any management flow can be broken into object, state, owner, timing, evidence and risk.

01

Business object

Customer, vehicle, order, store

02

State

Where it is now, and what comes next

03

Owner

Who owns it, who collaborates, who approves

04

Timing

When it starts, when it's due, whether it's late

05

Evidence

Records, photos, audio, outcomes

06

Risk

Rule alerts, AI judgment, escalation rules

Beliefs

What we believe

  1. AI's first job isn't drawing. It's deciding which data deserves trust.是决定哪些数据值得相信。
  2. Seeing risk isn't enough. The action must reach the right role.必须把动作送到正确岗位。
  3. Automation must be explainable and auditable; high-risk actions keep humans and owners in the loop.高风险动作保留人工与 Owner 确认。
  4. Prove it in real operations first, gate with contracts, then talk about scale.再用契约把门，再谈规模化开通。
