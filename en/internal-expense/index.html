Subscription assets

See every recurring
cost as it happens

Put software, services, and everyday subscriptions into one asset list. Who pays, who uses, and when it renews stay in context with terms and cycles.

Versioned terms
Keep a timeline of subscription changesso the current deal is reviewable.
Payer and user ownership

Renewal rhythm

Reimbursement loop

From candidate costs
to traceable batches

Capture pending costs as candidates first, then group them by payer into batches. FX snapshots, approval, and payment status stay on the record, so origin and destination stay clear.

  1. 01Record candidate costsCollect first.Do not break the existing flow.
  2. 02Batch by payerItems for the same payerbecome a checkable batch.
  3. 03Keep status and snapshotsRecord approval, payment, and FX timingfor a full replay path.
